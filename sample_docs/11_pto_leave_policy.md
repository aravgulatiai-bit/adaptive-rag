# Paid Time Off and Leave Policy

Northwind Analytics offers a competitive time-off program to full-time
employees. This policy summarizes vacation, holidays, sick leave, and parental
leave.

## Vacation

Full-time employees accrue 20 days of paid vacation per year. Up to 5 unused
vacation days may be carried over into the next calendar year; days beyond that
are forfeited. Vacation requests should be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance
through the HR portal.

## Company holidays

Northwind observes 10 paid company holidays each year, published in advance for
each region. Employees in Dublin and Singapore follow their local public holiday
calendars.

## Sick leave

Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year. Sick leave does not carry over.
For absences longer than 3 consecutive days, a doctor's note may be requested.

## Parental leave

Northwind provides 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave for the primary
caregiver and 8 weeks for the secondary caregiver, available to all full-time
employees regardless of gender, following 6 months of employment.

## Bereavement and jury duty

Up to 5 days of paid bereavement leave is available for an immediate family
member. Jury duty is paid in full for the duration of service. Questions about
leave go to hr@northwind.example.
