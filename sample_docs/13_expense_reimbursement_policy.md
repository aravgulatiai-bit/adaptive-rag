# Expense Reimbursement Policy

This policy explains how employees submit business expenses and how quickly they
are reimbursed.

## General rules

- Expenses must be reasonable, business-related, and submitted within 30 days.
- Receipts are required for any expense over 25 US dollars.
- Approved reimbursements are paid within 14 days of approval, in the next
  payroll cycle.

## Travel

- Air travel should be booked in economy class for flights under 6 hours;
  premium economy is permitted for flights of 6 hours or more.
- Hotel stays are reimbursed up to 250 US dollars per night in standard cities
  and up to 350 US dollars per night in high-cost cities.
- Ground transportation and standard ride-share services are reimbursable.

## Meals

Daily meals while traveling are reimbursed up to 75 US dollars per day.
Alcohol is not reimbursable except at pre-approved client entertainment events.

## Approvals

Expenses under 1,000 US dollars are approved by the employee's manager. Expenses
of 1,000 US dollars or more require director approval. Fraudulent expense claims
are grounds for termination. Submit expenses through the finance portal;
questions go to finance@northwind.example.
