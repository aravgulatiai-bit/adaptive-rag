# InsightHub Product Overview

InsightHub is Northwind Analytics' flagship cloud analytics platform. It lets
teams connect data sources, build dashboards, and set up automated alerts
without writing code.

## Core capabilities

- **Connectors**: over 120 pre-built connectors to databases, warehouses, and
  SaaS applications such as Salesforce, Snowflake, and PostgreSQL.
- **Dashboards**: drag-and-drop dashboards with real-time refresh.
- **Alerts**: threshold and anomaly-based alerts delivered by email, Slack, or
  webhook.
- **Governance**: row-level security, audit logs, and role-based access control.

## Subscription tiers

- **Starter**: up to 5 users, 10 data sources, community support.
- **Growth**: up to 50 users, 50 data sources, email support, SSO available.
- **Enterprise**: unlimited users, unlimited data sources, 99.9 percent uptime
  service level agreement, dedicated customer success manager, and a signed Data
  Processing Agreement.

## Data residency

Enterprise customers can choose to have their data stored in the United States,
the European Union (Frankfurt), or Singapore. Data does not leave the selected
region except as required to deliver the service and as described in the DPA.

## Availability

InsightHub targets 99.9 percent monthly uptime for Enterprise customers. Status
and historical uptime are published at status.northwind.example.
