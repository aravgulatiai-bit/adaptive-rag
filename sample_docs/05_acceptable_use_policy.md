# Acceptable Use Policy

This Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) governs how employees and contractors may use
Northwind Analytics systems, networks, and the InsightHub platform.

## Permitted use

Company systems are provided for legitimate business purposes. Incidental
personal use is permitted so long as it does not interfere with work, consume
significant resources, or violate any policy.

## Prohibited activities

- Sharing account credentials or bypassing multi-factor authentication.
- Installing unapproved software on company laptops.
- Accessing customer data without a documented business need (the principle of
  least privilege applies at all times).
- Disabling security controls such as disk encryption or anti-malware.
- Using company resources to send spam, harass others, or infringe copyright.

## Data handling

Confidential and customer data must never be copied to personal devices,
personal cloud storage, or removable media. Data classified as Restricted may
only be accessed from managed devices on approved networks.

## Enforcement

Violations of this policy may result in disciplinary action up to and including
termination, and may be reported to law enforcement where illegal activity is
involved. Questions about acceptable use should be directed to the IT service
desk at ithelp@northwind.example.
