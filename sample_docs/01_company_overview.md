# Northwind Analytics — Company Overview

Northwind Analytics, Inc. is a business-to-business software company that
provides a cloud analytics platform called InsightHub. The company was founded
in 2014 and is headquartered in Austin, Texas, with additional offices in
Dublin, Ireland and Singapore. As of 2025 Northwind employs approximately 600
people across engineering, sales, customer success, and operations.

The chief executive officer is Dana Whitfield, who co-founded the company. The
chief technology officer is Marcus Lee, and the chief information security
officer (CISO) is Priya Nair. The company is privately held and backed by
venture investors, having raised a Series C round of 80 million US dollars in
2022.

## Mission

Northwind's mission is to help mid-market and enterprise customers turn raw
operational data into decisions in minutes rather than weeks. The product is
sold primarily through an annual subscription model with three tiers: Starter,
Growth, and Enterprise.

## Business facts

- Fiscal year runs from February 1 to January 31.
- The company serves more than 2,000 paying customers in 40 countries.
- Annual recurring revenue crossed 120 million US dollars in fiscal year 2025.
- The primary cloud provider is Amazon Web Services, with data residency options
  in the United States, the European Union (Frankfurt), and Singapore.
