# Code of Conduct

Northwind Analytics is committed to a respectful, inclusive, and ethical
workplace. This code applies to all employees, contractors, and anyone acting on
the company's behalf.

## Core expectations

- Treat colleagues, customers, and partners with respect and professionalism.
- Do not engage in harassment, discrimination, or bullying of any kind.
- Avoid conflicts of interest, and disclose any that arise to your manager.
- Protect confidential and customer information at all times.

## Anti-bribery and gifts

Employees may not offer or accept bribes or kickbacks. Business gifts must be
modest; any gift valued over 100 US dollars must be declared to the compliance
team. Facilitation payments are prohibited.

## Diversity and inclusion

Northwind is an equal-opportunity employer. Hiring, promotion, and compensation
decisions are based on merit and never on race, gender, age, religion, disability,
sexual orientation, or other protected characteristics.

## Reporting concerns

Employees can report suspected violations to their manager, to HR, or
anonymously through the ethics hotline. Northwind prohibits retaliation against
anyone who raises a concern in good faith. Reports can be sent to
ethics@northwind.example.
