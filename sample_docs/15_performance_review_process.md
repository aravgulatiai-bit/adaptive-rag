# Performance Review Process

Northwind Analytics runs a structured performance review process to give
employees clear, fair, and regular feedback.

## Cadence

Formal performance reviews happen twice per year: a mid-year review in July and
a year-end review in January. Informal check-ins between managers and their
reports are expected at least every two weeks.

## Rating scale

Employees are rated on a 5-point scale:

1. Below expectations
2. Partially meets expectations
3. Meets expectations
4. Exceeds expectations
5. Outstanding

## Calibration

To ensure consistency and fairness, managers participate in a calibration
session where ratings across teams are reviewed together before they are
finalized. This reduces individual manager bias.

## Promotions and compensation

Promotion decisions and annual compensation adjustments are made during the
year-end cycle and take effect on March 1. A promotion requires a demonstrated
track record at the next level and manager plus skip-level approval.

## Performance improvement

Employees rated Below expectations are placed on a performance improvement plan
(PIP) with clear goals over a defined period, typically 60 days. Questions about
the process go to hr@northwind.example.
