# Privacy and Data Protection Policy

Northwind Analytics processes personal data as both a data controller (for its
own employees and website visitors) and a data processor (on behalf of customers
who use InsightHub). This policy describes the safeguards that apply.

## Legal bases and roles

For customer data processed through InsightHub, the customer is the data
controller and Northwind is the data processor. Processing is governed by a Data
Processing Agreement (DPA) that is incorporated into every Enterprise contract.
The DPA includes the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses for
international data transfers.

## Data protection officer

Northwind has appointed a Data Protection Officer (DPO) who oversees compliance
with GDPR, CCPA, and other applicable privacy laws. The DPO can be reached at
dpo@northwind.example and reports directly to the General Counsel.

## Key commitments

- Personal data is processed only for documented, legitimate purposes.
- Data minimization is applied: only the fields required for a feature are
  collected.
- Cross-border transfers rely on Standard Contractual Clauses or an adequacy
  decision.
- Sub-processors are listed publicly and customers are notified at least 30 days
  before a new sub-processor is added.
- Data breaches affecting personal data are reported to the relevant supervisory
  authority within 72 hours, consistent with GDPR Article 33.

## Cookies and website

The corporate website uses only strictly necessary cookies by default.
Analytics cookies are loaded only after the visitor gives consent through the
cookie banner. Consent choices are stored for 12 months.
