# Customer Support and Service Level Agreement

Northwind Analytics provides tiered customer support with response-time
commitments that depend on the subscription tier and the severity of the issue.

## Support channels

- **Starter**: community forum and email support.
- **Growth**: email support during business hours.
- **Enterprise**: 24/7 support by email and phone, plus a dedicated customer
  success manager.

## Response-time targets (Enterprise)

- **Critical** (production down): first response within 1 hour.
- **High** (major feature impaired): first response within 4 hours.
- **Normal** (general issue): first response within 1 business day.
- **Low** (question or feature request): first response within 2 business days.

## Uptime service level agreement

For Enterprise customers, InsightHub commits to 99.9 percent monthly uptime. If
uptime falls below this level, affected customers are eligible for service
credits: 10 percent of the monthly fee for uptime between 99.0 and 99.9 percent,
and 25 percent for uptime below 99.0 percent.

## Escalation

Customers can escalate an unresolved critical issue by replying to the support
ticket with the word ESCALATE, which pages the on-call manager. Support can be
reached at support@northwind.example.
