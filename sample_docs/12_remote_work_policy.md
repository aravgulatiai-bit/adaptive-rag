# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

Northwind Analytics operates a hybrid work model that balances flexibility with
in-person collaboration.

## In-office expectations

Employees who live within 50 kilometers of a Northwind office are expected to
work from the office at least 2 days per week. Fully remote roles are available
for certain positions and are designated at the time of hiring.

## Equipment and stipend

Northwind provides a company laptop to every employee. Remote and hybrid
employees receive a one-time home-office stipend of 500 US dollars and a monthly
internet reimbursement of 50 US dollars.

## Security requirements

Remote work must be performed on a company-managed device. Employees must use
the company VPN when accessing internal systems from untrusted networks, keep
full-disk encryption enabled, and never allow family members to use company
devices. Access to data classified as Restricted is only permitted from managed
devices on approved networks.

## Working across regions

Employees who wish to work from a location outside their home country for more
than 30 days per year must obtain approval from HR because of tax and compliance
implications. Questions go to hr@northwind.example.
