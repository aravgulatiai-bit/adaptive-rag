# Benefits and Compensation Overview

Northwind Analytics offers a comprehensive benefits package to full-time
employees, effective on the first day of employment.

## Health and insurance

- Medical, dental, and vision insurance, with Northwind covering 90 percent of
  the premium for employees and 75 percent for dependents.
- Company-paid life insurance equal to one year of base salary.
- Employee assistance program offering confidential counseling.

## Retirement

Northwind offers a 401(k) plan for US employees with a company match of 100
percent of the first 4 percent of salary contributed. Employees are fully vested
in the match immediately. Employees in Ireland and Singapore receive equivalent
locally compliant retirement contributions.

## Equity

Most full-time employees receive stock options as part of their offer. Options
vest over 4 years with a 1-year cliff, meaning 25 percent vests after the first
year and the remainder vests monthly thereafter.

## Learning and wellness

Every employee receives an annual professional development budget of 1,500 US
dollars for courses, conferences, and books. A monthly wellness stipend of 50 US
dollars can be used for gym memberships or fitness apps.

## Reviews

Compensation is reviewed once per year during the annual review cycle. Questions
about benefits go to hr@northwind.example.
