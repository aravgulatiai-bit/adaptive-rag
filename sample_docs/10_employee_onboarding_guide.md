# Employee Onboarding Guide

Welcome to Northwind Analytics. This guide summarizes what new employees can
expect during their first 90 days.

## Before day one

The IT team ships a company laptop so it arrives before the start date. Laptops
come with full-disk encryption, single sign-on, and required software
pre-installed. Accounts are provisioned with baseline access based on the new
hire's role.

## First week

- Complete security awareness and privacy training (required within the first 5
  business days).
- Set up multi-factor authentication on all accounts.
- Meet with your manager to review the 30-60-90 day plan.
- Be assigned an onboarding buddy from your team.

## First 90 days

New employees follow a structured ramp with clear milestones at 30, 60, and 90
days. Engineers ship their first small change to production within the first two
weeks, supported by a mentor and code review.

## Required training

All employees must complete security awareness training within 5 business days
and annually thereafter. Employees who handle customer data complete additional
privacy training covering GDPR and CCPA obligations.

## Getting help

The IT service desk (ithelp@northwind.example) helps with equipment and access.
People-team questions go to hr@northwind.example.
