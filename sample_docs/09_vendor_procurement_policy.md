# Vendor and Procurement Policy

This policy governs how Northwind Analytics selects, approves, and manages
third-party vendors, with particular attention to vendors that handle customer
or personal data.

## Security review

Any vendor that will store, process, or access Northwind customer data must pass
a security review before a contract is signed. The review covers the vendor's
certifications (such as SOC 2 or ISO 27001), data handling practices, and
sub-processor list. Vendors handling personal data must sign a Data Processing
Agreement (DPA).

## Approval thresholds

- Purchases under 5,000 US dollars per year require manager approval.
- Purchases between 5,000 and 50,000 US dollars require director approval and a
  security review if data is involved.
- Purchases over 50,000 US dollars require VP approval and a formal procurement
  process with at least two competing bids.

## Ongoing management

Critical vendors are re-assessed annually. A vendor register is maintained by
the procurement team, recording the owner, contract renewal date, and data
classification handled. Vendors that fail a re-assessment are placed on a
remediation plan or offboarded.

## Contacts

Procurement requests are submitted through the finance portal. Questions go to
procurement@northwind.example, and security reviews are coordinated with
security@northwind.example.
